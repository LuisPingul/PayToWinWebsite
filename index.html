The Unholy Plague & Covenant Ark World Lore

After the original biblical stone tablets are uncovered, the Pope grasps the artifact—binding Satan’s shackles as firmly as when God first cast him down. In retaliation, Hell descends upon the mortal world, unleashing an unholy plague that turns mankind into demonic zombies.

Conventional survival supplies (food, water, and trauma kits) can be scavenged from ruined settlements, but blessed bullets and sacred wards can only be replenished through liturgical prayer—leaving the five-person divine retinue vulnerable during prayer windows as you escort the Covenant Ark toward the Pope’s sanctuary.

Risk vs. Economy: Every Bullet Has a Price Core Loop

Across seven escalating nights inside a 360° Holographic Combat Zone, every infected kill awards Salvage Cash ($). Wave completion triggers a timed 30-second Buying Phase / Respite Sanctuary intermission window.

Press B to open the Salvage Armory with three tactical tabs: Workbench & Ammo (weapon unlocks, 5-tier gun part upgrades, and ammo packs), Field Stims (bought directly into inventory up to 5 stacks), and 500-Point Ballistic Armor Plates. Every dollar spent on firepower is a dollar taken from your family’s ICU survival fund.

FAMILY ICU STAKES & AFTERMATH SYSTEMS

Family ICU Ladder, Expense Tally & 8 Endings v2.0.67

Player survival without financial restraint is deliberately framed as a tragedy: reaching deep rounds only to bleed out with empty pockets leaves every ICU bed unpaid. Three interconnected UI systems enforce this core identity in real time and after death:

Top-Left Live ICU Ladder FamilyGoalLadderHUD

Docked top-left during combat. Rises and turns Emerald (SAVED) as cash climbs; immediately turns Crimson (SPENT) whenever Armory spending drops you below a bed you previously reached.

"Who Have You Saved?" Tally PlayerDeathHUD

Upon death, clicking WHO HAVE YOU SAVED? opens a categorized ledger itemizing Total Earned, Weapons, Upgrades, Ammo, and Armor & Stims against all 7 ICU hospital bills.

"The Story After You Died" 8 Unique Endings

Computes which beds survived from final leftover cash (0/7 up to 7/7) and presents a tailored epilogue naming exact survivors, lost members, Day reached, and unspent payout.

Option B: Build & Run from Unity Source

1

Install Unity 2022.3.62f3 LTS

Open Unity Hub and install Unity 2022.3.62f3 LTS with the Windows Build Support (IL2CPP or Mono) module.

2

Load Main Survival Scene

Open the project in Unity Hub, navigate to Assets/_Project/Scenes/World_OpenSurvival_Map.unity in the Project window, and open the scene.

3

Play in Editor or Build Standalone (x86_64)

Press Play in the Editor, or go to File → Build Settings → Windows (x86_64) and click Build And Run.
